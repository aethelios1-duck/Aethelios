Long before the pickaxes of [[Brimstone Hollow]] echoed into the night, the earth beneath it was a barren, scorched wasteland known as the Wailing Chasm. It was the lair of **Vorkan**, a primordial dread-titan born from the dark sea of Chaos.

Vorkan was not merely a monster; it was an engine of wild, chaotic magic. Its skin absorbed spells like water, and its beating heart pumped endless, molten mana through its veins, threatening to unmake the realm and tear down Olympus itself. Neither the thunderbolts of Zeus nor the trident of Poseidon could pierce its monstrous hide. Every drop of blood it shed sprouted into wild arcana that shattered reality.

Seeing that the beast could neither be destroyed nor tamed, the Gods assembled a cosmic anvil. Guided by Hephaestus, they forged a weapon of ultimate suppression: a monolithic spike of solid **Adamantine**—the pitch-black, indestructible metal forged from the heart of dead stars, capable of consuming and nullifying all magic in existence.

When the beast roared, shattering the mountains, Zeus parted the storm clouds, and Apollo cast a beam of blinding light to pin the titan in place. Down from the heavens fell the Adamantine Spike, driven straight through the heart of Vorkan like a cosmic stake.

The force of the strike buried the titan deep underground, collapsing the surrounding stone and carving out the colossal void known today as **[[the abyss]]**.

As the massive stake pierced the titan’s heart, a dead silence fell over the world. The absolute magic-nullifying properties of the Adamantine seized the titan's core. Its heart could no longer produce or pump mana. Deprived of its magical lifeblood, Vorkan fell into an endless, unyielding coma. Over centuries, the ambient pressure of the world fused the titan’s calcified mana and the bleeding edges of the celestial Adamantine stake into the deep, rich veins of metal that run through the walls of the Abyss today.

Thus, Brimstone Hollow was born. Miners dig deep into the darkness, carving out precious shards of the black alloy to forge legendary weapons and anti-magic armor—all while treading lightly upon the cliffs of the Abyss. For every soul in the city knows the unwritten truth: they are not just mining a metal reserve. They are slowly chipping away at the very cage that keeps the nightmare below asleep.
