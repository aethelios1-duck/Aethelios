the abyss of [[Brimstone hollow]], the source of the most powerful metal known to man adamantine. [[the mining sector]] is built around the abyss separating it from rest of the city.

in the year 1355 AD the abyss was dried up because of over mining by the residents of the city. that caused the core of [[Vorkan]] to reawaken causing a plague to spread, and the mana the core produced made the people who died from the plague to reawaken and rise as undead. the mana even spread to the river and ocean affecting the merfolk that live there.
