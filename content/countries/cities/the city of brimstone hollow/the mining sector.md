the city of [[Brimstone hollow]] was built in two sectors [[the residential sector]] and the mining sector, with two walls one to protect the whole city "the outer wall", and a second to separate the mining sector and the residential sector "the inner wall".

the mining sector was where all the metal work and blacksmiths resided. it's separate form the rest of the city by "the inner wall". it also is the only entrance to the abyss the massive cave system that was rich with the most powerful metal known to man the metal of the gods adamantine.

it also the place where the famous blacksmith Olaf lives in his blacksmith shop Hephaestus's hammer.
