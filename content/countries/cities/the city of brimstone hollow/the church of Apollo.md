# the history of the church of Apollo in brimstone hollow.

---

the church of Apollo was built in the year 1295 AD. it was both a place of worship and a place of medicine for the poor and sick. it was founded by great bishop father Klein, he was a great leader he was almost chosen by the oracle of Delphi to be the Pope it was ether him or Pope Leo and of course we all know who was chosen.

at the year 1333 AD [[father Julius]] a local priest was promoted to being the bishop of the church. during his time as the bishop he was the one who suggested to build the slums outside the north gate when the homeless problem got out of hand. he also was the one that found the prodigy of medicine alter.

in the year 1355 AD the church of Apollo found out that the newly started plague in the city seems to be reanimating the corpses of the people were infected by it. alter was placed in charge of doing research on the undead, she started theorizing that the reason the corpses get reanimated is not the plague but a large amount of mana being poured into it, the source of the mana is unknown.
