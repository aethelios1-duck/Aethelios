the city of brimstone hollow was built in two sectors the residential sector and the mining sector, with two walls one to protect the whole city "the outer wall", and a second to separate the mining sector and the residential sector "the inner wall".

the residential sector was not originally built with to different districts "upper district and lower district". they came to be recently because of the massive divide between the wealth of the residents of the city, where the richer of the residents live closer to the outer wall and further then the mining sector, while the poorer of the residents lives closer to the wall of the mining sector and some even live in the mining sector in there shops or blacksmiths.
