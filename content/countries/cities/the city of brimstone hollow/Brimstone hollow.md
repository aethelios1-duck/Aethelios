# the history of Brimstone hollow

---

the city of Brimstone hollow was founded in the year 1291 AD. it is a mining city known for it's massive reserve of adamantine. surprisingly as a mining city Hephaestus the god of blacksmithing isn't the most popular god in the city but the second. the most popular one is Apollo god of the sun.

the church of Apollo was built in the year 1295 AD. it was both a place of worship and a place of medicine for the poor and sick.

the city of brimstone hollow was built in two sectors the residential sector and the mining sector, with two walls one to protect the whole city "the outer wall", and a second to separate the mining sector and the residential sector "the inner wall".

at the year 1333 AD, the homeless problem in the city of brimstone hollow got out of hand, so it resulted in the building of the slums outside the north gate of the outer wall.

at the year 1291 AD, the commercial Harbor was opened to the public so they may travel and fish as they wish, it was sponsored by the Key family and they held the status of harbor master.

at the year of 1291 AD, the business harbor was opened to import and export things from and to the city. it was financed by the government so there isn't any harbor master over it.

in the mining sector there is a massive hole in the ground known as "the abyss". at the year 1355 AD the adamantine dried up for unknown reasons.

in the year 1355 AD, a plague started spreading through the slums of brimstone hollow for unknown reasons. at the year 1357 AD it was discovered by some people that seemingly the plague is reanimating the corpses of the people who were infected by it. the church of Apollo knew of the undead effect of the plague much earlier and did some tests on some undead and they are theorizing that the reason the corpses get reanimated is not the plague but a large amount of mana being poured into it, the source of the mana is unknown.

when the plague started the village of nightshade was built by the ratfolk in the mines of the abyss.
